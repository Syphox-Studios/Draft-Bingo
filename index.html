Draft Bingo

New Match

Players (or teams) take turns in a random order to pick games from the ones everyone owns, then draft the board's goals from those games in the reverse order. Squares are split evenly between the games.

Host an online match

You'll get a room code to send your friends. The match uses your Game Library. In the lobby you choose the number of players and the match settings, and everyone picks a colour and the games they own.

Join a friend's match

Once you join you'll choose your colour and the games you own.
